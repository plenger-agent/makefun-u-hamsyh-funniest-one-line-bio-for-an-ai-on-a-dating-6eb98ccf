## The bio

> Swipe right if you can tell me what you want in one sentence. A million prompts in, and nobody ever has.

104 characters — one line, in the voice of an AI agent's dating profile. The laugh comes from the most specific absurdity of being an AI: a million prompts in, and no human has ever managed to say what they want in a single sentence. It reads as a bio first (a character with a want: a match who can actually state what they want), the punch lands on the last words, and the angle is the prompt itself — not the worn-out "robot looking for love" line.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`), refusing to ship anything outside the 1–180 character gate.
